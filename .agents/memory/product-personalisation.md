---
name: Product personalisation
description: Storewide customer personalisation pricing and fulfilment requirements.
---

Offer personalisation automatically on every product, including newly added products: No or Yes, then independent Name and Number choices. Charge AED 10 per name and AED 10 per number, or AED 20 for both, per physical item.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested automatic availability on products and automatic pricing at checkout.

**How to apply:** Keep personalisation optional and server-priced. Distinct names/numbers must remain separate bag lines. Preserve the entered text with the order so staff can fulfil it. Do not add the fee twice when displaying an inclusive unit price.

Every shopper-facing add-to-cart entry point must use the same personalisation flow; shortcut buttons must not silently bypass it.

**Why:** Wishlist initially added products directly, bypassing the new customer choices.

**How to apply:** When adding any product quick-add action, route through the shared quick-view controls rather than sending a bare cart request.
