---
name: Amazon Back to School sourcing
description: Rules for refreshing Back to School products from public Amazon listings.
---

**Rule:** Back to School products use verified Amazon.com listing titles, Amazon-hosted product images, and private Amazon source URLs. Convert the displayed USD listing price to AED at 3.6725, then add an AED 10–20 margin before setting the storefront price.

**Why:** The collection was explicitly changed from generated placeholders to real Amazon products, with prices that preserve a small AED markup while retaining provenance for Admin review.

**How to apply:** Refresh only when a listing exposes a matching title, product image, current price, and direct Amazon URL. Keep the provenance URL restricted to verified Admin product reads; never expose it in public product payloads or substitute an unverified/generic listing.
