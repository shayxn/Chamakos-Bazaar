---
name: News publishing intent
description: The owner's expected behavior when adding news in Admin.
---
The owner expects news added in Admin to appear directly below the main homepage hero.

**Why:** The owner reported that news did not work because an ordinary save silently left the story as a draft.

**How to apply:** Make publishing the clear default for new news, with an explicit save-draft option. Keep intentionally saved drafts, archived news, and future-scheduled stories out of the public feed. Do not apply the news-specific default to other content types.
