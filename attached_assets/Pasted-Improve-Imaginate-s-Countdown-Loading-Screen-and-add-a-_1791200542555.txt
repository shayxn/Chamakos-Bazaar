3. When the video finishes, smoothly fade to black.
4. Display:
“We know you’re excited, just a few moments!”
Small text below:
“This may take a few minutes.”
5. Load/prepare the website behind this screen.
6. As soon as the website is genuinely ready, smoothly fade the message out and fade the website in.
7. Do NOT use a fake multi-minute delay. If the site is ready quickly, continue quickly.
8. If no launch video is uploaded, skip directly to the post-countdown loading/launch sequence.
9. Make sure late visitors do not unnecessarily replay the launch video every visit.
