==========================================================
IMAGINATE — SECRET WORLDWIDE SHIPPING & COUNTRY MANAGEMENT
==========================================================

Add an advanced WORLDWIDE SHIPPING system to the existing Imaginate Admin.

IMPORTANT:

THIS FEATURE IS SECRET / DISABLED BY DEFAULT.

DO NOT ENABLE WORLDWIDE SHIPPING.

DO NOT ACTIVATE IT DURING DEVELOPMENT.

DO NOT AUTOMATICALLY ENABLE IT AFTER AN UPDATE.

WORLDWIDE SHIPPING MUST REMAIN:

OFF

until the OWNER explicitly enables it.

==========================================================
OWNER ONLY
==========================================================

Create:

ADMIN
→ SETTINGS
→ SHIPPING & REGIONS
→ WORLDWIDE SHIPPING

Only the OWNER can enable or disable Worldwide Shipping.

Normal Admins may only access country/shipping settings if the Owner gives them the appropriate permission.

The master Worldwide Shipping switch itself should remain OWNER ONLY.

Display:

WORLDWIDE SHIPPING

[ OFF ]

Underneath:

SECRET FEATURE

Do not enable unless worldwide shipping is ready.

==========================================================
CURRENT DEFAULT
==========================================================

While Worldwide Shipping is OFF:

Imaginate remains primarily configured for the UAE.

Primary currency:

AED — UAE DIRHAMS

The existing UAE shopping experience should continue working normally.

Do not change the current UAE store simply because this worldwide system was added.

==========================================================

WORLDWIDE SHIPPING

[ CONTINUE ]

==========================================================

ON

==========================================================
IMPORTANT — EXISTING ORDERS
==========================================================

Disabling a country must NEVER delete previous orders from that country.

Historical orders must remain accessible.

Example:

If 20 customers previously ordered to the United States and Owner later disables United States shipping:

KEEP those 20 orders.

Only prevent NEW orders to that destination.
